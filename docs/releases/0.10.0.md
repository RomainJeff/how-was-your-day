# 0.10.0

Recording works with a Bluetooth headset microphone.

- A Bluetooth headset used as microphone switches to call mode and runs at 16 or 24 kHz, while system audio stays at 48 kHz. Recording used to refuse to start (« Fréquences différentes »). The microphone and system audio now run on two separate device clocks: HWYD aligns both tracks on host time and compensates their drift, one sample at a time, beyond 2 ms.
- With matching rates (built-in microphone, USB interfaces), capture is unchanged.
- When buffers are lost, the error now gives the cause counters.

Drift correction is validated on synthetic audio (1000 ppm, alignment within 3 ms) and on short real sessions with a Bluetooth headset. Long-session drift on real devices has not been measured yet.
