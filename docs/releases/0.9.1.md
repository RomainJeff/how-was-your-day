# 0.9.1

Clearer error when a recording cannot start because of the audio layout.

- The message « Le format ou l’ordre des pistes n’est pas pris en charge » is replaced by the exact check that failed and the values read: channel count, stream order, sample format, or sample rates of the microphone and system audio. A sample rate mismatch also suggests choosing another microphone or aligning the rates in Audio MIDI Setup.

Recording behaviour is unchanged: a layout the capture pipeline cannot handle still stops before anything is recorded.
