# 0.9.0

Voice identity is simpler to use and every step can be reversed.

- **Nouvelle session** opens a setup screen: choose the session type, optionally a name, then start recording. Nothing is captured before you press the button.
- App messages are now stacked notifications in the corner of the window. Information leaves after a few seconds; a notification that asks for a decision carries its buttons (Réessayer, Réglages, Afficher) and stays until answered.
- The « Identité des voix » panel is gone. Everything happens on the voice tiles: **Mémoriser cette voix…** (with confirmation) for a named voice, **Nommer et mémoriser…** otherwise. Recognition runs again on the session after each enrollment.
- No more channel to choose. A voice print now has two channels, microphone and call, deduced from the track. A person known on only one of them can be suggested on the other, above a stricter threshold (0.85 instead of 0.75). Former « Proche » and « Salle » samples are merged into the microphone channel automatically.
- Voice corrections (name, regroup, link, ignore) show **Annuler** in their notification. A voice merged into another one can be split back later with **Séparer** in the tile menu.
- Each stored voice sample now remembers the session it came from. In Settings › Audio et voix, open a person to see what each session added and **Retirer** it, **Effacer l’empreinte** without blocking the person, **Effacer et opposer**, or **Lever l’exclusion** (a new enrollment is then required).

Samples stored before 0.9 have no known session; they are listed together and can be withdrawn as a group. Voice matching thresholds are still not calibrated on a representative corpus.
